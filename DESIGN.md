# Visual direction
The user explicitly chose the existing mental-math-trainer theme system. Its theme catalog and three CSS sources are copied verbatim into `dist/vendor/`; their SHA256 hashes match the originals. Default Carbon Paper, all 59 palettes, Studio/Opaline styles, and independently selectable Light/Dark/Adaptive modes are retained. Tokens are applied using the same camelCase-to-CSS-variable mapping as the original SiteLayout.
Newsreader is the body face, Syne is the interface/heading face, and IBM Plex Mono carries elapsed time. A 224px navigation column becomes a compact header on mobile. The stopwatch is the signature working control; subject colors encode identity. Timer and tasks share the first area, heatmap and subject totals follow. Surfaces, controls and browser chrome derive from the copied semantic tokens. No brand icon or favicon.
Operate mode. Show meaningful empty/error/success states. No photography, illustrations, logo icon, favicon, marketing hero or extra product scope.
