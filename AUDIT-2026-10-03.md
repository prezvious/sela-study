# Project bug audit — 3 October 2026

Reviewed the current working tree, including the uncommitted account-sync implementation. This is an audit of the maintained application, local server, schema, and verification code. Existing edits and application source were preserved. **All ten findings below have now been fixed and verified.** See [FIX-VERIFICATION-2026-10-03.md](FIX-VERIFICATION-2026-10-03.md) for the corrections and current regression coverage.

**Original audit result: ten confirmed bugs.** Eight were P2 functional/recovery defects; two were P3 display/navigation defects. Each finding had a concrete reproduction. The original passing suite did not cover these cases; maintained regressions now cover the corrected behaviour. The descriptions and source locations below record the code at the time of the audit.

## Confirmed findings

### A101 — Stale task and subject dialogs overwrite newer edits (P2)

**Location:** [app.mjs:189](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:189); the same problem affects the subject editor at [app.mjs:182](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:182).

**Trigger:** Tab A opens an existing task. Tab B changes its title and saves. A receives the storage update, then saves a date change using the still-open draft. The corresponding subject case changes the colour in A after B renames the subject.

**Actual:** A's date change succeeds but resets B's title to the old draft title. The subject colour change similarly resets B's new name. No conflict or backup is presented.

**Cause:** The editors read the latest state under the lock, then assign every submitted field from the old form without checking the original record or distinguishing edited fields from unchanged draft values. Serializing writes does not prevent this lost update.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-stale-ui-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` executes the real modal submit handlers. The task title changes from `Updated in tab B` back to `Old title`, and the subject name changes from `Renamed in tab B` back to `Math`.

**Recommended correction:** Compare original field values with the latest record inside the lock. Preserve newer fields the user did not edit, and reject conflicting edits to the same field.

### A102 — A synchronous storage-read failure permanently stops sync retries (P2)

**Location:** [sync.mjs:80](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/sync.mjs:80).

**Trigger:** An attached account encounters one synchronous `storage.getItem()` failure during the initial `read()` inside a flush. Storage subsequently becomes readable.

**Actual:** Repeated forced syncs do nothing. The test account remains at revision 1 with zero tasks even though the server is at revision 2 with a new task. The engine stays in its previous error state until its account generation is reset or the page reloads.

**Cause:** The async IIFE runs synchronously until its first `await`. An initial read exception reaches `finally`, which clears `task`; then the outer assignment stores the already-settled promise back into `task`. Every later flush returns that promise at line 74.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-sync-read-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` injects one temporary read failure, restores reads, and performs two forced flushes. The newer server document is never adopted.

**Recommended correction:** Assign the task promise before beginning work, for example by deferring the body through a promise continuation. Cleanup must also clear the task when failure precedes the first asynchronous boundary.

### A103 — A midnight DST transition makes weekly history disagree with totals (P2)

**Location:** [i18n.mjs:53](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/i18n.mjs:53), consumed by [app.mjs:86](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:86).

**Trigger:** Use `America/Santiago`, a Monday-first locale, and a current date of 6 September 2026. Record a session from 00:00 to 00:30 on Monday 31 August.

**Actual:** Weekly statistics show `00:30:00`, while history says there are no sessions. Sessions partly overlapping the faulty boundary also receive a shortened history duration.

**Cause:** Midnight on 6 September normalizes to 01:00. `localeWeekStart()` moves that Date to the previous Monday while retaining 01:00. Aggregation uses only its date and includes Monday's first hour; history filtering and clipping use the full timestamp and exclude that hour.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-dst-history-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` reports a week start of `Mon Aug 31 2026 01:00:00` and asserts both the thirty-minute total and empty history table.

**Recommended correction:** Recompute the local start of the target weekday after changing its civil date. Keep the history and aggregation boundaries identical.

### A104 — Sign-out leaves Account controls disabled (P2)

**Location:** [app.mjs:127](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:127).

**Trigger:** Sign in, remain on the Account page, then select Sign out.

**Actual:** The guest sign-in form appears with its email, password, and submit controls disabled. They remain disabled after the next ten-second poll. Navigating again re-enables them. An operation failure can also leave rendered account controls in their busy state.

**Cause:** Auth notifications render the guest form while `cloudPending` is still `out`. The operation's `finally` clears that flag but re-renders only Settings, excluding the Account view.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-signout-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` uses headless Edge and asserts that the email field is disabled immediately and eleven seconds later, then becomes enabled after navigation.

**Recommended correction:** Re-render Account after clearing the busy flag, as well as Settings.

### A105 — Account polling discards date-entry drafts and closes the picker (P2)

**Location:** [sync.mjs:101](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/sync.mjs:101), with [app.mjs:123](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:123) and [app.mjs:134](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:134).

**Trigger:** While signed in, type a partial date into the Study page's task-date field, or open its calendar picker, and wait for the normal remote poll. The cloud document has not changed.

**Actual:** The partial date is replaced with the previously selected date. The open picker disappears. This also affects date pickers in modal forms because `render()` closes the shared picker.

**Cause:** Every successful pull writes and publishes the workspace even when the document is unchanged, triggering a full render. Draft preservation covers Settings/Account inputs only; rendering always closes the picker.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-date-poll-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` uses Edge: `03/` changes back to `03/10/2026` after a poll, and an open picker disappears after another poll.

**Recommended correction:** Avoid publishing an unchanged workspace, and preserve an actively edited date and picker across necessary background renders.

### A106 — Successful forced sync leaves an obsolete conflict active (P2)

**Location:** [sync.mjs:101](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/sync.mjs:101) and [sync.mjs:116](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/sync.mjs:116).

**Trigger:** Two devices conflict on a task title. The other device subsequently changes its title to match the local version. Select Sync now on the device that detected the conflict.

**Actual:** The device adopts the matching revision 3 and reports `synced`, but retains the revision-2 conflict and its obsolete remote title. The next ordinary poll reports `conflict` again and further ordinary pulls stop at the conflict guard.

**Cause:** Successful reconciliation does not clear `conflict`. Only account attachment and explicit conflict resolution clear it.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-sync-conflict-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` records `synced` with a still-present conflict, a current revision of 3 versus a conflict revision of 2, then `conflict` on the following flush.

**Recommended correction:** Clear the previous conflict when an authoritative reconciliation completes successfully; ensure cross-tab resolution can also invalidate stale conflict state.

### A107 — A valid task date can make the whole workspace unreadable in another timezone (P2)

**Location:** [core.mjs:100](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/core.mjs:100).

**Trigger:** A workspace contains a task dated `2011-12-30`, created or imported in UTC or Singapore. Open that workspace in `Pacific/Apia`, which skipped that civil date.

**Actual:** The same document passes validation in UTC and fails with `error.backupFormat` in Apia. A guest workspace enters recovery; account documents fail cloud validation. Unrelated records become inaccessible through the normal workspace.

**Cause:** Task date validation round-trips a date-only string through a local timestamp. A valid Gregorian date becomes the next local date when the local timezone skipped midnight/the entire date. Validity of the whole document therefore depends on the receiving device's timezone.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-boundaries-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` validates the identical JSON in separate UTC and Apia processes and asserts the differing results.

**Recommended correction:** Validate and preserve date-only task values using timezone-independent civil-date rules. Handle local calendar gaps separately from document validity.

### A108 — Invalid account-cache metadata bypasses the recovery UI (P2)

**Location:** [app.mjs:293](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:293), following [sync.mjs:70](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/sync.mjs:70).

**Trigger:** The study document is valid but its `_sync` metadata is invalid, for example a negative revision. This is a synthetic cache-corruption case, not a normal edit produced by the app.

**Actual:** Sync correctly rejects the cache. The app then loads it through the study-only validator, clears its unreadable-data state, and offers no original-byte download/start-fresh recovery controls. A subsequent edit appears in memory but fails persistence and is misleadingly reported as full/unavailable storage.

**Cause:** When sync initialization fails, `onWorkspace(record:null)` falls back to `restoreLocalState()`, which ignores `_sync`. The app and engine disagree about whether the account cache is readable. Generic persistence error handling mislabels the metadata rejection.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-metadata-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` asserts the missing recovery action, the storage-full message, an in-memory goal of 30, and an unchanged persisted goal of 240.

**Recommended correction:** Validate the entire account cache in the account recovery path and expose safe metadata repair/reinitialization while preserving the study document and original bytes. Preserve the actual failure reason.

### A109 — The setup guide's Back link leaves a subdirectory deployment (P3)

**Location:** [guide.mjs:8](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/guide.mjs:8).

**Trigger:** Publish the app at a path such as `https://example.test/sela/`, open its setup guide, and select Back.

**Actual:** The link goes to `https://example.test/` rather than the application's directory. That can open another site or a 404.

**Cause:** The return link is hard-coded to `/`. The app otherwise uses relative assets and documents support deployed subpaths.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-boundaries-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` renders the actual guide module with a subdirectory URL and asserts the incorrectly resolved root destination.

**Recommended correction:** Resolve the app link relative to the guide's directory. Review equivalent root-relative links/assets before using the error page on a subdirectory host.

### A110 — Subject lifetime totals stop updating while its timer runs (P3)

**Location:** [app.mjs:91](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:91) and [app.mjs:262](C:/Users/camar/Documents/Coding/sela-study/dist/app.mjs:262).

**Trigger:** Start a guest stopwatch, open Subjects, and remain there for at least two minutes.

**Actual:** The timer continues, but the subject's displayed lifetime total remains at its initial value indefinitely until another render/navigation. A signed-in account happens to refresh it through polling, which does not correct the guest workflow.

**Cause:** Subjects initially include the active interval through `allSessions()`, but `tick()` does not update these totals or render the Subjects view.

**Evidence:** `work/audit-stale-ui-repro-2026-10-03.mjs` advances the actual UI clock by 120,000 ms and invokes the real tick handler; the lifetime total stays at `0m`.

**Recommended correction:** Update the displayed lifetime totals at an appropriate cadence while a timer is running.
